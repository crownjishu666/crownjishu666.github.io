隐私政策

最后更新：2026年9月29日

我们重视你的隐私

欢迎使用坦克大冒险pg（英文名 classic tank）。这是一款可离线游玩的单机街机坦克游戏。本政策说明应用会处理哪些信息、这些信息如何保存，以及你可以如何管理它们。

我们的隐私承诺

  • 无需注册账号，不收集姓名、手机号、邮箱或其他身份信息
  • 战绩与语言偏好只保存在你的设备上，开发者无法读取
  • 不接入广告、统计、崩溃上报或跨应用追踪
  • 不出售数据，也不根据你的游戏行为做广告画像
  • 卸载应用即可清除本机保存的全部数据

1. 我们的基本原则

• 本地优先：游戏过程与记录都在设备上完成
• 数据最小化：只保存记住战绩和语言设置所必需的内容
• 无账号：没有登录、服务器账户或云端个人档案
• 透明：本政策按当前版本的实际功能撰写；若以后增加会改变数据处理方式的功能，我们会先更新本政策

2. 我们处理的信息

2.1 保存在本机的游戏记录

一局结束（胜利或失败）后，应用会在本机记下战绩，用于档案页展示。这些数据包括：

  • 最高分、单局最高击毁数
  • 总对局数、胜利次数、总击毁数，以及据此算出的胜率
  • 最近最多 30 条对局记录：日期、得分、击毁数、胜负结果和记录时间

这些内容不会上传，也不会与其他玩家同步排行榜。

2.2 语言偏好

  • 你在档案页选择的语言：跟随系统、简体中文或 English
  • 若选择“跟随系统”，应用只会在本机读取系统首选语言，用来决定界面显示中文还是英文

2.3 我们不收集的信息

  • 姓名、身份证件、电话号码、电子邮箱、通讯录
  • 精确或粗略位置、相机、麦克风、相册
  • 广告标识符、跨应用追踪标识
  • 账号、密码、支付信息
  • 使用统计、崩溃日志，或任何发送到开发者服务器的诊断数据

主界面出现的活动提示是应用内的玩法介绍，不是第三方广告，也不会据此识别或追踪你。

3. 数据如何保存

3.1 仅本地保存

战绩和语言偏好只写在你的设备上。开发者没有服务器接收这些数据，也无法远程查看、修改或恢复它们。

3.2 系统备份

如果你开启了设备备份，操作系统可能把应用的本地数据包含进你自己的备份。该备份由你和设备厂商管理，不会因此发送给本应用的开发者。

3.3 应用商店

从 App Store 下载或更新应用时，Apple 会按其自身隐私政策处理与商店相关的信息。该处理由 Apple 负责，不属于本应用收集的数据。

4. 我们如何使用这些信息

  • 在档案页显示最高分、胜率和对局历史
  • 记住你选择的界面语言
  • 维持单机对局、关卡和暂停等核心玩法

我们不会把这些信息用于广告、出售、用户画像，或与第三方共享。

5. 我们的承诺

  • 不出售你的数据
  • 不进行广告追踪或跨应用画像
  • 不建立可识别个人身份的用户档案
  • 当前版本不向开发者或任何第三方传输游戏数据

6. 数据共享

就本应用自身而言，我们不与第三方共享你的游戏记录或语言设置。

  • 没有广告联盟、分析服务或推送服务接入
  • 没有社交登录或排行榜服务器
  • 开发者不持有你的游戏记录副本。若法律要求披露信息，该义务适用于实际持有数据的一方

7. 数据安全

  • 数据留在你的设备上，并受设备锁屏与系统安全机制保护
  • 应用不提供需要上传这些记录的网络通道
  • 请自行保管设备。他人解锁你的设备后，可以在本机档案页看到战绩

8. 你的权利

因为数据只在本机，你可以直接管理它们：

  • 在档案页查看战绩与历史记录
  • 在档案页随时更改语言
  • 卸载应用以删除本机保存的战绩和设置

卸载后，这些本地记录无法由开发者恢复。若设备备份中仍留有副本，需在系统的备份设置中另行处理。

9. 本地偏好与分析

应用使用本机存储记住语言和战绩，不使用 Cookie，也不在应用之间追踪用户。当前版本不包含使用统计或诊断上报。

10. 儿童隐私

本应用是适合休闲游玩的单机游戏，不会故意收集儿童或任何用户的个人身份信息。我们不要求创建账号，也不请求姓名、联系方式或位置。

11. 数据保留

  • 战绩与语言设置：一直保留在本机，直到你卸载应用或清除应用数据
  • 对局历史：本机最多保留最近 30 条，更早的记录会在新对局写入时被替换
  • 开发者侧：不保留你的游戏数据副本，因此也不存在服务器端保留期限

12. 政策更新

如果应用增加账号、联网对战、云存档、广告或统计等会改变数据处理方式的功能，我们会更新本政策，并修改页面顶部的“最后更新”日期。更新生效后继续使用应用，即表示你了解更新后的政策。

13. 联系我们

本应用没有应用内反馈账号，开发者也不持有你的个人数据副本。
如对本政策有疑问，请通过 App Store 应用页面上的开发者联系方式与我们联系。

你的游戏记录留在自己的设备上。

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 29, 2026

Your Privacy Matters

Welcome to classic tank (坦克大冒险pg). This is an offline, single-player arcade tank game. This policy explains what information the app processes, how that information is stored, and how you can manage it.

Our Privacy Commitments

  • No account is required, and we do not collect your name, phone number, email, or other identity information
  • Scores and language preference stay on your device — the developer cannot read them
  • No advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or cross-app tracking
  • No data selling and no advertising profiles based on how you play
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all data stored by the app on this device

1. Our Core Principles

• On-device first: gameplay and records stay on your device
• Data minimization: only what is needed to remember scores and language
• No account: no sign-in, server account, or cloud profile
• Transparency: this policy matches the current version; we will update it before adding features that change how data is handled

2. Information We Process

2.1 Game Records Stored on Your Device

When a match ends in a win or a loss, the app saves the result locally for the profile screen. This includes:

  • High score and the most kills in a single match
  • Total matches, wins, total kills, and the win rate calculated from those figures
  • Up to the 30 most recent matches: date, score, kills, result, and record time

This information is not uploaded and is not synced to a leaderboard.

2.2 Language Preference

  • The language you choose on the profile screen: follow system, Simplified Chinese, or English
  • If you choose “follow system,” the app reads the device’s preferred language locally only to decide whether the interface is shown in Chinese or English

2.3 What We Do Not Collect

  • Name, government ID, phone number, email address, or contacts
  • Precise or coarse location, camera, microphone, or photo library
  • Advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking identifiers
  • Account credentials or payment information
  • Usage statistics, crash logs, or any diagnostic data sent to the developer

The promotional card on the main screen introduces gameplay. It is not a third-party advertisement and is not used to identify or track you.

3. How Data Is Stored

3.1 On This Device Only

Scores and language preference are written only on your device. The developer has no server that receives this data and cannot view, change, or restore it remotely.

3.2 Device Backup

If you turn on device backup, the operating system may include the app’s local data in your own backup. That backup is managed by you and the device vendor. It is not sent to the developer of this app because of that backup.

3.3 App Store

When you download or update the app from the App Store, Apple processes store-related information under Apple’s own privacy policy. That processing is Apple’s responsibility and is not data collected by this app.

4. How We Use Data

  • Show your high score, win rate, and match history on the profile screen
  • Remember the interface language you selected
  • Run single-player matches, stages, and pause

We do not use this information for advertising, sale, profiling, or sharing with third parties.

5. Our Commitments

  • We do not sell your data
  • We do not perform ad tracking or cross-app profiling
  • We do not build a profile that identifies you
  • The current version does not transmit game data to the developer or any third party

6. Data Sharing

This app does not share your game records or language setting with third parties.

  • No ad network, analytics service, or push service is integrated
  • There is no social sign-in and no leaderboard server
  • The developer does not hold a copy of your game records. If the law requires disclosure, that duty applies to the party that actually holds the data

7. Data Security

  • Data stays on your device and is protected by the device lock screen and system security
  • The app does not provide a network channel for uploading these records
  • Keep your device secure. Anyone who unlocks it can see scores on the profile screen

8. Your Rights

Because the data stays on your device, you can manage it directly:

  • View scores and match history on the profile screen
  • Change the language on the profile screen at any time
  • Uninstall the app to delete locally stored scores and settings

After uninstall, the developer cannot restore these local records. If a copy remains in a device backup, remove it in the system backup settings.

9. Local Preferences and Analytics

The app uses on-device storage to remember language and scores. It does not use cookies and does not track you across apps. The current version does not include usage statistics or diagnostic reporting.

10. Children's Privacy

This is a casual single-player game. It does not knowingly collect personal identity information from children or from any user. It does not ask anyone to create an account, and it does not request a name, contact details, or location.

11. Data Retention

  • Scores and language settings remain on the device until you uninstall the app or clear its data
  • Match history keeps at most the latest 30 records; older records are replaced when a new match is saved
  • The developer does not keep a copy, so there is no server-side retention period

12. Policy Updates

If the app adds accounts, online play, cloud save, advertising, analytics, or other features that change how data is handled, we will update this policy and the “Last Updated” date at the top of this page. Continued use after an update takes effect means you have read the updated policy.

13. Contact Us

This app has no in-app feedback account, and the developer does not hold a copy of your personal data.
If you have questions about this policy, contact us through the developer contact shown on the App Store listing.

Your game records stay on your device.
